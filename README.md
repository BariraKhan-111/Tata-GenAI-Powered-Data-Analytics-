# Geldium Delinquency Prediction & AI-Powered Collections Strategy

### Overview
Geldium is a fictional financial services company facing rising customer
delinquency. This project simulates the role of a data analytics / AI
consultant brought in to help Geldium move from reactive debt collection to
a proactive, data-driven, and responsible approach — using customer data to
predict risk early and designing an AI system that can act on that risk at
scale.

### Problem Statement
Geldium's collections process currently treats most customers the same way,
relying on outreach *after* an account is already delinquent. This is
costly, inconsistent, and often comes too late to prevent the account from
deteriorating further. Geldium needs a way to:

- Identify which customers are likely to become delinquent **before** it
  happens, using demographic, financial, and payment-behavior data
- Act on that insight through timely, targeted interventions instead of a
  one-size-fits-all approach
- Scale these interventions through automation — without sacrificing
  fairness, transparency, or regulatory compliance

### Objective
Design an end-to-end approach that:

1. **Predicts delinquency risk** using a model built on customer data
2. **Translates that risk into actionable recommendations** for collections
   and business stakeholders
3. **Scopes a responsible AI system** that can apply these insights
   autonomously, with clear guardrails for fairness, explainability, and
   human oversight

### Approach
The project was carried out as a series of structured tasks — data
exploration, predictive model design, stakeholder-facing recommendations,
and an AI system strategy — each building on the last. Task-specific
descriptions and deliverables are included separately in this repo.

### Tools & Skills Used
- Data analysis & predictive modeling concepts (classification, imbalanced
  data, model evaluation)
- GenAI-assisted drafting, reviewed and corrected by hand
- Business communication: translating technical output into stakeholder
  recommendations and an executive-style strategy presentation
- Responsible AI principles: fairness, explainability, and regulatory
  compliance in a financial services context

##### Disclaimer
This project was completed as part of an educational virtual job
simulation. "Geldium" and the underlying dataset are fictional/simulated
and do not represent a real company or real customer data.
